# Cancellation and Changes Policy

## Cancelling a booking
You can cancel a booking at any time before departure, free of charge. Cancelling releases your seat for other riders, so please cancel as soon as you know you won't travel.

## Changing your departure time
You can move a booking to another departure on the same route and day if that departure has a free seat and has not passed its 30-minute booking cut-off. The Earlier and Later buttons and the Clear Path assistant both make this change for you.

## Missed shuttles and no-shows
If you don't cancel and miss your shuttle, it counts as a no-show. Three no-shows within 30 days pause new bookings for 7 days. Cancelling, even at the last minute, never counts as a no-show.
