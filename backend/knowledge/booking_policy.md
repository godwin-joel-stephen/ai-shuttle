# Booking Policy

## Service days
Clear Path shuttles run Monday to Friday. There is no service on Saturdays or Sundays, so weekend bookings are not accepted. If you need to travel on a weekend, book the Friday before or the Monday after instead.

## Booking window
Bookings open 14 days before the travel date. A date further ahead than that cannot be booked yet; the app tells you the day booking opens. This keeps seats fair for everyone and lets operations plan shuttle capacity.

## Same-day bookings
You can book a shuttle for today until 30 minutes before its departure. After that, booking for that departure is closed because the driver's passenger list is finalised. A later departure on the same route may still be open.

## Seat availability
Each shuttle has 10 seats and seats are first come, first served. If your usual shuttle is full, choose another departure on the same route; Clear Path shows which departures still have seats. Morning shuttles leave every 15 minutes between 7:00 AM and 8:00 AM.

## One booking per route per day
You can hold one booking per route per day. Asking to book the same day again returns your existing booking instead of reserving a second seat. To travel at a different time, change your existing booking.
