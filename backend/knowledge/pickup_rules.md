# Pickup Rules

## Arriving at the stop
Be at your pickup stop at least 5 minutes before departure. Drivers wait up to 2 minutes after the scheduled departure time, then leave to keep the rest of the route on schedule.

## Pickup and drop-off points
Morning shuttles pick up at the Home stop and drop off at the Office main entrance. Evening shuttles run the reverse route from the Office to the Home stop.

## Delays
If your shuttle is running more than 10 minutes late, Clear Path notifies you and suggests the next departure that still gets you there on time.

## Luggage and accessibility
Each rider may bring one bag and one personal item. All shuttles have step-free access; tell the driver if you need the ramp.
