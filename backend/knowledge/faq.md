# Frequently Asked Questions

## Why does it say I'm already booked?
You already hold a booking on that route for that day. Clear Path never books two seats for the same trip; change the existing booking's time instead.

## What is my usual shuttle?
Your usual shuttle is the departure you take most often, currently the 7:30 AM Home to Office shuttle. Booking "my usual shuttle" always picks it; you can still choose any other departure.

## Can I book for someone else?
No. Bookings are personal and tied to your account, because drivers check riders against the passenger list.

## Do shuttles run on public holidays?
No. Public holidays follow the company calendar and have no service, the same as weekends.
