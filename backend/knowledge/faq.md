# Frequently Asked Questions

## Why does it say I'm already booked?
You already hold a booking on that route for that day. Clear Path never books two seats for the same trip; change the existing booking's time instead.

## What is my usual shuttle?
Your usual shuttle is learned for each weekday from your recent rides, for example the departure you took on most of your last five Mondays. If you pin a shuttle as your usual, the pin wins. With no ride history it is the 7:30 AM Home to Office shuttle. Booking "my usual shuttle" picks it; you can still choose any other departure.

## Can I book a shuttle for someone else, such as a colleague, friend or guest?
No. Bookings are personal and tied to your account, because drivers check riders against the passenger list. A colleague who needs a seat books it from their own account.

## Do shuttles run on public holidays?
No. Public holidays follow the company calendar and have no service, the same as weekends.
